FAQ

How many guests can an event have?

The Entire Studio fits 60 to 100 people. Studio 1 fits up to 60 and Studio 3 fits 25 to 30.

What comes with a studio booking?

The dressing room and the full equipment list: lights, stands, softboxes, reflectors, backdrops, tripods, sliders, Bluetooth mics and speaker, a projector and a rolling smart TV.

Can I bring outside food and drinks?

Yes, with no corkage. Please clean as you go.

How can I pay?

Cash, GCash, credit and debit cards, and bank transfer.

We're planning a big shoot or a long booking. Who do we talk to?

Email hello@nestworkspaces.com with the details. Our head manager will get back to you with the best offer she can give.
