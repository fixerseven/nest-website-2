Inside Studio 1: what you can shoot on a 12 by 4 meter infinity wall

A quick guide to our cyclorama studio: the space, the kit that comes with it and how to book.

Nest Workspaces · October 2026 · 3 min read

Studio 1 is the biggest of our three studios: 58.5 square meters with a white cyclorama that curves from the wall into the floor, 12 meters wide and 4 meters deep. The curve hides the corner, so the background reads as endless white on camera.

What it's good for

  • Product and e-commerce shoots on a clean white background
  • Lookbooks, fashion and campaign photos
  • Team and group portraits, with room for up to 60 people
  • Video interviews, ads and content days

What comes with every booking

You don't need to bring lights. Every studio booking includes:

  • Godox SL60 and SL200 lights and a Nanlite 300W light
  • Octagon and rectangle softboxes, and 3 Bowens-mount reflector dishes
  • 3 C-stands and backdrop stands in various sizes
  • 5 wooden backdrops: 2 white, light pink, light blue and green
  • 2 sliders, a ball head tripod and a fluid head tripod
  • 2 Bluetooth mics, a Bluetooth trolley speaker, a projector and a rolling smart TV
  • Foldable tables and chairs, plus the 10.8 sqm dressing room with a lit mirror counter

How to book

Send your date, start time, hours and what you're shooting through the studio booking form, and we'll confirm availability. Planning a long shoot or a series? Email hello@nestworkspaces.com with the details and our head manager will put together an offer.
