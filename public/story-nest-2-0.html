Nest 2.0 arrives November 3. Here's what's new.

Same nest at Horizons 101, with a new look, every rate in one place and easier booking.

Nest WorkspacesOctober 20262 min read

Nest is getting a refresh. The space stays where it is, at B1 Horizons 101 on Gen. Maxilom Ave, with the same 7 AM to 1 AM hours and 24/7 access for members. What changes is how it looks and how easy it is to book.

What's new

  • A new website with every rate on it. Day passes, Flexi Passes, memberships, private offices, meeting rooms and studios, all with prices. No more messaging to ask how much.
  • Booking from any page. Request a tour, a meeting room, a studio or an event date from the site, and we'll confirm availability.
  • A page for meeting rooms. Our 6-seat and 12-seat rooms now have their own page, with what's included and how to book.
  • A fresh look across our website and socials, built around one idea: do the next thing.
  • Stories from the flock. A home for member stories and guides like this one.

What stays the same

  • Day Pass ₱400, from 7 AM to 1 AM, with unlimited coffee, tea and water.
  • 300 Mbps internet, free parking and an ergonomic chair.
  • Members get in 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
