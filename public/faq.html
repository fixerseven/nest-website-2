Visiting

What are your opening hours?

Walk-ins and day passes: daily from 7 AM to 1 AM. Members have access 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Where are you, and is there parking?

We're at B1, Horizons 101 Condominiums on Gen. Maxilom Ave, Cebu City. Parking is free for members and guests. Directions and map.

How fast is the internet?

Download speed is 300 Mbps and upload runs 45 to 60 Mbps, on PLDT and DataConnect.

Can I bring outside food and drinks?

Yes, with no corkage. Please clean as you go.

What's in the community pantry?

Unlimited free coffee, tea and water, a microwave you can use, and a few drinks for sale.

How can I pay?

Cash, GCash, credit and debit cards, and bank transfer.

Do you have discounts?

Students get 10% off. PWD and senior citizens get 20% off.

Coworking and offices

Do you have hourly desk rates?

There's no hourly desk rate. The Day Pass is ₱400 and covers 7 AM to 1 AM, with free coffee, water and tea, high-speed internet, free parking and an ergonomic chair.

Can I book a private booth for an hour?

Yes. A private booth is ₱150 an hour on top of your Day Pass.

Can I leave my things overnight?

Members can. We don't recommend leaving gadgets or other valuables in the open area overnight.

Can I set up my private office my way?

Yes. Bring your own furniture and decorate with our approval, as long as nothing could damage the walls.

Meeting rooms

What comes with a meeting room?

Free coffee, water and tea, high-speed internet, free parking, a smart TV with an HDMI cable and a whiteboard.

Do members get meeting room hours?

Yes. Every membership includes 3 hours of meeting room use each month.

How do I book a meeting room?

Send the form on the meeting rooms page, message us on Messenger or call (0945) 451 0789 with your date, start time and number of people. We'll confirm availability.

Studios and events

What comes with a studio booking?

The dressing room and the full equipment list: lights, stands, softboxes, reflectors, backdrops, tripods, sliders, Bluetooth mics and speaker, a projector and a rolling smart TV.

How many guests can an event have?

The whole studio floor fits 60 to 100 people. Studio 1 fits up to 60 and Studio 3 fits 25 to 30.

We're planning a big shoot or a long booking. Who do we talk to?

Email hello@nestworkspaces.com with the details. Our head manager will get back to you with the best offer she can give.
