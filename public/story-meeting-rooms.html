Which meeting room fits your meeting?

Six seats or twelve, an hour or a whole afternoon. A quick guide to booking a room at Nest.

Nest Workspaces · October 2026 · 2 min read

Nest has two meeting rooms you can book by the hour, plus bigger spaces for talks and events. Every booking includes a smart TV with HDMI, a whiteboard, high-speed internet, free coffee, tea and water, and free parking.

Small Meeting Room: up to 6 people

₱400 an hour. Right for interviews, client calls, tutoring sessions and study groups.

Large Meeting Room: up to 12 people

₱900 an hour. Right for team meetings, workshops, trainings and presentations.

Need more chairs?

Extra seats are ₱200 per person, per hour.

Bigger than a meeting

For a talk or a screening, the amphitheater in the coworking space starts at ₱2,000. For 60 to 100 guests, book the Entire Studio, from ₱14,000 for 4 hours.

Members, use your hours

Every membership includes 3 hours of meeting room use each month.

How to book

Send your date, start time, hours and headcount on the meeting rooms page, message us on Messenger or call (0945) 451 0789. We'll confirm availability and the total.
