FAQ

Is Nest open 24 hours for studying?

Walk-ins are welcome daily from 7 AM to 1 AM on a ₱400 Day Pass. If you need to stay overnight, the Owl Pass gives you 24 hours for ₱550. Members have 24-hour access every day.

Do students get a discount?

Yes. Students get 10% off. PWD and senior citizens get 20% off.

Is there an hourly rate?

There's no hourly desk rate. The Day Pass covers the whole day from 7 AM to 1 AM, so you can stay as long as you need.

Can I bring food?

Yes, with no corkage. There's a microwave in the pantry, and coffee, tea and water are free and unlimited.

Where is Nest, and is there parking?

B1, Horizons 101 Condominiums, Gen. Maxilom Ave, Cebu City. Parking is free. Directions and map.
