When a small business, a startup or a remote team in Cebu City outgrows the kitchen table, the usual next step is to look at office space for rent. A traditional lease means deposits, fit-out, furniture, internet installation and utility bills before anyone sits down. For teams of one to five, a serviced private office or a coworking membership is often simpler and cheaper.

The three options

  • A traditional lease. Your own unit, your own rules, but usually a long contract and setup costs you pay up front.
  • A private office in a coworking space. A lockable room for your team, with internet, furniture, coffee and meeting rooms already there.
  • Coworking memberships. A desk for each person in a shared space, with 24-hour access.

Choose a private office if

  • You're on calls most of the day, or handle client information that needs a closed door.
  • You want to leave monitors, files and equipment set up.
  • You want the space to feel like yours. At Nest you can bring your own furniture and decorate with our approval, as long as nothing damages the walls.

Choose coworking memberships if

  • Your team is often out, or comes in on different days.
  • You're one or two people and want to keep costs low while you grow.
  • You like working around other people, and the events and introductions that come with a community.
