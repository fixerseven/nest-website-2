If you've been working from home, a café or a condo lobby, a coworking space gives you a proper desk, fast internet and a reason to get dressed in the morning, without signing a long office lease. This guide walks through how coworking works at Nest Workspaces on Gen. Maxilom Ave, Cebu City, what each option costs and who it suits.

How coworking works

You pay for access, not for a whole office. Some people come in for a single day. Others use a few days a month, and some make Nest their everyday base with their own desk or a private office. Everything shares the same building, the same pantry and the same 300 Mbps internet.

At Nest there's no hourly desk rate. A Day Pass covers the whole day, from 7 AM to 1 AM, so you never have to watch the clock.

Option 1: drop in for a day or a week

Good for trying the space, a deadline week, or when you just need to get out of the house.

  • Day Pass, ₱400. 7 AM to 1 AM, every day of the week.
  • Owl Pass, ₱550. A full 24 hours, for night owls and people working with clients in other time zones.
  • Week Pass, ₱1,500. Seven days in a row, 7 AM to 1 AM.

No appointment needed. Walk in, and the front desk will set you up.

Option 2: Flexi Passes for a few days a month

If you come in two or three times a week, a Flexi Pass is a bundle of days you use whenever you like before it expires. A Flexi 7-Day Pass is ₱2,490, which works out cheaper than seven separate Day Passes. There are bigger bundles too, listed on the coworking page.

Option 3: a monthly membership

Once you're in most weekdays, a membership is the better deal. A Hotdesk Membership is ₱5,500 a month and lets you take any open desk. A dedicated desk is yours alone, and you can leave your things there overnight.

Every membership includes:

  • 24-hour access with your own keycard
  • 3 hours of meeting room use every month
  • 10 color and 20 black-and-white prints every month
  • 6 guest hours every month
  • Free or discounted community events

Option 4: a private office or booth

For a small team, or anyone who takes a lot of calls, a private office closes the door on the noise. Offices fit 1 to 3 people and start at ₱20,000 a month for 2 people, with each extra person at ₱5,500. You can bring your own furniture with our approval. Read private office or coworking membership if you're deciding between the two.

What's included with every pass

  • 300 Mbps internet on PLDT and DataConnect, with 45 to 60 Mbps upload
  • Unlimited coffee, tea and water
  • An ergonomic chair
  • Free parking at Horizons 101
  • Bring your own food with no corkage, and a microwave in the pantry

Students get 10% off. PWD and senior citizens get 20% off.

Which one should you pick?

  • Trying it out, or once in a while: a Day Pass.
  • Two or three days a week: a Flexi Pass.
  • Most weekdays, or late nights: a membership with 24-hour access.
  • A team of 2 or 3 who need privacy: a private office.
