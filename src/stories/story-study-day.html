A Day Pass at Nest is ₱400 and runs from 7 AM to 1 AM. That's 18 hours of desk time, with unlimited coffee, tea and water. Students get 10% off. Here's how to get the most out of it.

Start early

Doors open at 7 AM. Come in before the lunch crowd, pick a spot in the lounge or at a shared desk, and plug in. The internet runs at 300 Mbps download, so lecture videos and review apps won't stall.

Pack food, skip the lines

You can bring your own food and drinks with no corkage. There's a microwave in the community pantry, and coffee, tea and water are free all day. Please clean as you go.

Study as a group

The small meeting room seats up to 6, with a whiteboard and a smart TV with HDMI for slides. It's ₱400 an hour.

Going late

We're open until 1 AM. If your night runs longer, the Owl Pass gives you 24 hours for ₱550.

Reviewing for weeks?

Flexi Passes let you buy days in bulk and use them when you need them, from ₱2,490 for 7 days used within 30 days. If you're here most days, the Hotdesk Membership is ₱5,500 a month with 24-hour access.
